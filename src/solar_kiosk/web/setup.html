Solar panels (Enphase)

Choose how the solar app signs in to your Enphase gateway.

  1. Find your gateway The solar app looks for it on your home network. It doesn't sign in to anything.

    You can also see the serial number in the Enphase app: tap Menu (bottom right), then System, Devices, Gateway. It's the number after SN:

  2. Make a token on Enphase's website This is easiest on a computer. Your Enphase password goes only to Enphase, never to the solar app.
    1. Open entrez.enphaseenergy.com (it opens in a new tab).
    2. Click Login, then sign in with the email and password you use in the Enphase app.
    3. From the lists, choose your system's name, then your gateway's serial number.
    4. Click Create access token.
    5. In the window that opens, click Copy and close.
  3. Paste the token here

  4. Click ConnectThis checks that the token works.

A token lasts a year. About a month before it runs out, the solar page shows a reminder. Then repeat steps 2 to 4.

Something went wrong?

"Couldn't find an Enphase gateway": the solar app has to be on the same home network as the gateway. You can still type the serial number from the Enphase app in step 2, and the gateway's address under More options.

"Made with an installer's account": if you installed the system yourself, Enphase treats your account as an installer's, and its tokens only last 12 hours. Use Email and password instead, or ask Enphase Support to change your account to a homeowner account.

"The gateway didn't accept that token": check you chose this gateway's serial number in step 2, then make a new token and paste it again.

  1. Type the email and password you use in the Enphase app

    They're kept on the solar app's device, and sent only to Enphase, so the app can get a new token by itself every year. If your Enphase account asks for a code from your phone when you sign in, this won't work: use Token from Enphase instead.

  2. Click ConnectIt finds your gateway on your home network and checks the login. This takes up to a minute.

Battery (FranklinWH)

Uses the same login as the FranklinWH app.

  1. Type the email and password you use in the FranklinWH app

    The battery is read through FranklinWH's servers, so the solar app needs the internet. The login is kept on the solar app's device, and sent only to FranklinWH.

  2. Click ConnectIt signs in to FranklinWH and shows your battery's charge.

Weather (Tempest)

Found automatically. Nothing to type.

  1. Check the Tempest hub is plugged in and set up It should already be working in the Tempest app, on the same home network (the same Wi-Fi or router) as the solar app.
  2. Click Look for itThe solar app listens for the hub for up to 15 seconds.
