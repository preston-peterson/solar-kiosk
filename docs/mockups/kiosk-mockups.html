Everything shown is invented sample data. Hover the charts and panels for exact values. “Excess” is solar made minus what the home used today. The weather strip and the dashed “expected for the sunlight” line come from the Tempest weather station: when solar falls well below that line on a sunny day, something is wrong with the array; on a cloudy day both drop together and nothing alerts. The “Today so far” card cycles through this week, this month and this year every 6 seconds: click it (or its dots) to step through, hover to pause. “Kiosk preview” hides this bar and shows exactly what the TV would display; press Esc to come back. The page address remembers your choices, so reopening it returns to the same view.
